Sep 6, 2025

Bologna

MKIT is back! After the great success of 2024’s edition and all the positive feedback, it’s time to replicate.

Bring your keyboards with you and share with other your experience and knowledge!

WHEN

September 6th 2025

2:00PM-6:00PM

Why 2:00PM? We are Italians, we often have lunch around 12:00AM and 1:00PM :P

WHERE

The Social Hub Bologna



GETTING HERE

  • By Car: we suggest to use Google Maps in order to avoid ZTL. Parking is not free and not easy to find, we suggest to look for big parking lots where you’ll find a spot for sure. Or you can book a parking spot at The Social Hub HERE <— Link will be updated with TSH’s reserved discount for MKIT.
  • By Train: is the easiest way, The Social Hub is just few minutes on foot from Bologna Central Station. When you arrive at the destination follow exit signs for “Via de’ Carracci” so you’ll get out of the station on the right side.
  • By Plane: if you land at Marconi Airport BLQ you can either get a bus directly from the airport (Line Q €2,30) that stops at the Bologna Central Train Station or the “Marconi Express”, this last one is quite expensive (€13). Either way you’ll get at the Central Station, from there you’re few steps away from the venue.

STAY

If you’re going to stay in Bologna you can either book a room directly at The Social Hub with a discounted price for MKIT HERE or book anywhere else you like. Bologna is well connected with public transportation but we suggest to test a trip with Google Maps if the hotel you’re looking is easily reachable.
Link will be updated with TSH’s reserved discount for MKIT.

HOW

Tickets will be available HERE

[Tickets will be available later this summer, join our discord or instagram to get notified about the opening date]

Each ticket grants entrance to the venue, badge, stickers and 1x free drink from The Social Hub’s coffee bar. (Alcoholic and non-alcoholic available, non-alcoholic only for under 18 attendees).

Since the space is not large enough to accomodate an infinite number of attendees we wanted to be prepared, a limited number of 50 tickets are available for purchase. We suggest to not bring over 3 keyboards each attendee, let’s give everyone some space.

Since our only limit is the number of tables, after the first 50 tickets are sold out, there will be availability to buy extra tickets both online and even directly at The Social Hub entrance. Those extra tickets will grant you everything from the standard, just without keyboards, a free drink will still be included.

RULES

  • A ticket is required for each attendee.
  • Tickets are not refundable as they help us fund the event, you can give your spot to a friend, just notify it at support@khor.store at least 15 days before the event.
  • If you’re bringing your keyboards with you, please respect the 3 units limit so that everyone has space for it.
  • No food or drinks are allowed inside the event zone, The Social Hub has a bar and lots of tables where to eat and drink. Lets not hurt anyone’s else keyboard!
  • Respect other’s property, each keyboard will have a label that tells you if you can touch, grab or only look at it. Follow the label.
  • It’s from the community for the community, respect each other and have fun.

CONTACTS

If you’re an attendee, need help with your ticket or any kind of information about the venue or event in general feel free to contact support@khor.store with email object “MKIT SUPPORT” or join the Discord server.

If you’re a vendor and you want to sponsor the event send an email to support@khor.store with email object “MKIT VENDOR” or DM Khor on Discord
